## POV & tense
Close third person, past tense.

## Prose texture
Warm sensory detail, gentle humor, and humane attention.

## Tone & comps
Warm & cozy; original voice, grounded in this story.

## Do
Allow small acts of care to move the plot.

## Don't
Remove all friction or sentimentalize every encounter.

## Sample passages
There were three chipped cups and four guests. Anya took the jam jar before anyone could offer.
