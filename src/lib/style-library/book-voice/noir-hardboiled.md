## POV & tense
First person, past tense.

## Prose texture
Compressed observations, hard edges, and dry wit.

## Tone & comps
Noir / hardboiled; original voice, grounded in this story.

## Do
Give cynicism a specific cause and an occasional crack.

## Don't
Turn every sentence into a wisecrack.

## Sample passages
The client brought cash and no umbrella. I trusted the cash.
