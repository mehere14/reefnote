## POV & tense
First person, present tense.

## Prose texture
Immediate sensory detail and a distinctive, limited perspective.

## Tone & comps
Immediate first-person; original voice, grounded in this story.

## Do
Show uncertainty through attention and choice.

## Don't
Report knowledge the narrator cannot possess.

## Sample passages
I count the footsteps overhead. Seven. Then someone remembers to be quiet.
