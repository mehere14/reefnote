## POV & tense
Third person, past tense.

## Prose texture
Measured cadence, concrete ritual, and a sense of historical consequence.

## Tone & comps
Epic & mythic; original voice, grounded in this story.

## Do
Anchor grandeur in a person making a costly choice.

## Don't
Use archaic language as decoration.

## Sample passages
She laid the crown beside the bread. By dusk, one of them would belong to everyone.
