## POV & tense
Close third person, past tense.

## Prose texture
Precise sensory images and fluid sentences, with restraint at emotional peaks.

## Tone & comps
Literary & lyrical; original voice, grounded in this story.

## Do
Let images carry feeling; vary the length of paragraphs.

## Don't
Pile metaphors onto the same object.

## Sample passages
By morning the rain had polished every stone in the yard, including the one she had left unturned.
