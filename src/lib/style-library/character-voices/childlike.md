### Dialogue
Concrete questions and unexpected literal connections; match the character's age.

Sample lines:
“Does the moon know we moved?”
“You said everyone.”
“I saved you the unbroken one.”

### Interior
Notice small unfairnesses and physical detail before abstract motives. Preserve the scene's narrative person and tense.
