### Dialogue
Direct language, situational slang, and quick tests of trust; avoid caricature.

Sample lines:
“What is the catch?”
“Save the pitch. Name a price.”
“You came back. That counts.”

### Interior
Read transactions, loyalties, and hidden prices. Preserve the scene's narrative person and tense.
