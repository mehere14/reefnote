### Dialogue
Qualify claims, make distinctions, and occasionally lose the thread.

Sample lines:
“There are, strictly speaking, two possibilities.”
“That was the preliminary conclusion.”
“I had hoped you would ask.”

### Interior
Organize uncertainty into models; let private emotion interrupt analysis. Preserve the scene's narrative person and tense.
