### Dialogue
Self-corrections and tangents with a clear underlying want.

Sample lines:
“I checked it. Well, most of it.”
“Before you answer, one thing.”
“That sounded worse than I meant.”

### Interior
Rehearse consequences and search faces for reassurance. Preserve the scene's narrative person and tense.
