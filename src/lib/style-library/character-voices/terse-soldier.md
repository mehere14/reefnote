### Dialogue
Short concrete statements; practical questions; no obligatory jargon.

Sample lines:
“Door first.”
“Count again.”
“I said I would wait.”

### Interior
Notice exits, fatigue, risk, and obligations. Preserve the scene's narrative person and tense.
