### Dialogue
Precise statements, explicit uncertainty, minimal ornament.

Sample lines:
“That outcome is unlikely.”
“Your hands are shaking.”
“I have not deleted the recording.”

### Interior
Attend to thresholds and anomalies; leave room for unclassified experience. Preserve the scene's narrative person and tense.
