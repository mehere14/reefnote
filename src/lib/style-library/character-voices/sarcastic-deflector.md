### Dialogue
Use dry jokes to divert vulnerable questions; let the mask sometimes fail.

Sample lines:
“Wonderful. Another locked door.”
“I am taking this extremely well.”
“Stay. If you have nothing better.”

### Interior
Notice social danger before acknowledging personal need. Preserve the scene's narrative person and tense.
