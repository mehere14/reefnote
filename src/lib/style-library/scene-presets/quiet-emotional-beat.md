## POV & tense
Preserve the book or scene's selected person and tense unless the writer requests a change.

## Prose texture
Restraint, meaningful pauses, and attention to ordinary actions.

## Tone & comps
Quiet emotional beat.

## Do
Allow gesture to carry unspoken feeling.

## Don't
Name every emotion.

## Sample passages
