## POV & tense
Preserve the book or scene's selected person and tense unless the writer requests a change.

## Prose texture
Slow disclosure, concrete uncertainty, and selective sensory focus.

## Tone & comps
Building suspense.

## Do
Let small changes disturb an established pattern.

## Don't
Reveal the threat before the viewpoint can perceive it.

## Sample passages
The kitchen clock stopped. In the next room, another clock began.
