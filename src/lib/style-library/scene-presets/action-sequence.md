## POV & tense
Preserve the book or scene's selected person and tense unless the writer requests a change.

## Prose texture
Clear spatial cause and effect, short decisive beats, with moments of longer breath.

## Tone & comps
Action sequence.

## Do
Give every action a consequence.

## Don't
Lose track of positions.

## Sample passages
