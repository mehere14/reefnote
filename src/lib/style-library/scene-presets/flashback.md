## POV & tense
Preserve the book or scene's selected person and tense unless the writer requests a change.

## Prose texture
Associative transitions and selective remembered detail.

## Tone & comps
Flashback.

## Do
Make the temporal shift clear and return with changed meaning.

## Don't
Let a memory become a complete unrelated biography.

## Sample passages
The smell of hot copper brought back the workshop. Her father had still had both hands then.
