## POV & tense
Preserve the book or scene's selected person and tense unless the writer requests a change.

## Prose texture
Use the selected log, letter, or transcript form consistently.

## Tone & comps
In-world document.

## Do
Let omissions and form reveal the writer.

## Don't
Break the document frame without reason.

## Sample passages
Watch log, 03:10. No vessels sighted. The entry for 03:09 has been removed.
